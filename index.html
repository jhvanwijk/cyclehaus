The process

Simple from start to finish.

No surprises. We'll always tell you what's needed and what it costs before doing any work.

1

Drop off your bike

Bring it in at our Boston workshop. We'll take your details and log the job immediately.

2

We assess and report

Our technician inspects your bike and sends you a full diagnostic report — with photos — to your phone.

3

You approve online

Review each item, approve the work you want done, and leave a note if needed. No phone tag required.

4

Collect your bike

We'll notify you when it's ready. Pay on collection and ride away with confidence.
